Frequently asked questions

A few straightforward answers before you get started.

Do I need to pay to use Fenixpace?

No. You can create a free account and use the core Fenixpace experience, including planning, commitments, consistency tracking, and social features. Supporter memberships are optional.

What do the supporter memberships add?

Early Supporter adds a supporter badge and recognition. Power Supporter includes those benefits and removes ads. Family Member includes the Power Supporter benefits and lets you submit courses to Grow for review.

Where can I see the current prices?

Visit the Support page for current monthly prices and checkout options. This landing page does not process payments.

How can I contact Fenixpace?

You can email us at support@fenixpace.space or call us at +201200738657. You can also visit our Contact us page.
